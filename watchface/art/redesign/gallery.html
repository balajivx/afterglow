Round 2 · you picked Broadcast

Broadcast, four ways

More from the motorsport and football broadcast world: F1's timing tower and onboard graphics, TAG Heuer's race-weekend faces, Sky Sports and Premier League score bugs, and the crisp type of Apple's 2026 F1 coverage. Motorsport italics throughout.

A

Race Control

The Broadcast you liked, refined

Borrowed from

F1 TV graphics and Apple's 2026 F1 coverage: slanted italics, a red graphics tag for the date, a lap counter ("DAY 324/366").

What changes

Heart rate and battery become two chips at the top. Steps, calories and FPL become segmented energy-store bars. The caption gets a second tier: "LIVE · TEST · DAY 2/5" above the score.

On the watch

Bars light segment by segment, the same technique as the step lights. Battery: same as today.

B

Timing Tower

Your day as F1's leaderboard

Borrowed from

The timing tower down the side of every F1 broadcast: position number, team-colour chip, three-letter code, gap.

What changes

Steps, calories, heart rate, battery and FPL become five rows of the tower under a header ("FRI 20 NOV · D324"). The time stacks big on the left, hour over minute.

On the watch

Text and small solid shapes only: the cheapest to draw of the four. Battery: same or slightly lower (dark panel).

C

Onboard

The in-car view: shift lights and a readout

Borrowed from

F1 onboard graphics and the steering wheel: shift lights from green to red to purple, a speed readout, gear boxes.

What changes

Your step lights become a real F1 shift-light arc that fills towards your goal. The time is the readout, heart rate and battery sit in gear-style boxes, and calories and FPL are delta chips.

On the watch

The face already has step lights; these are recoloured and reshaped. Battery: same.

D

Score Bug

The live score up top, a ticker at the foot

Borrowed from

Sky Sports and Premier League score bugs; the news ticker along the foot of a sports channel.

What changes

The sports line becomes a TV score bug at the top (sport tab, both scores, a red LIVE flag, the Test's days underneath). The time is huge and centred, and all five stats run in a ticker along the bottom.

On the watch

The score bug is built from the same widget data as today's sports line. Battery: same.

Round 1

The first four directions

Today's face. Eight elements at roughly the same weight: date, time, capsule, three dials, the pill and a busy photo. The redesigns pick one hero, the time, and give everything else a clear, quieter place.

1

Broadcast

The F1 TV lower third, on your wrist

Borrowed from

F1 TV and Sky Sports on-screen graphics; TAG Heuer's F1 edition. Condensed type, timing-board readouts.

What changes

A huge condensed time, flush left. Steps, calories and FPL become slim gauges on the right. The sports line is a TV caption bar with a team-colour tab.

On the watch

All static drawing, like today. Gauges are lit dashes, the same technique as the step lights. Adds one font (Barlow Condensed, open licence). Battery: same.

2

Concentric

Rings on the edge, a calm centre

Borrowed from

Pixel Watch's Concentric and Apple's Activity rings: data lives on the bezel, the middle stays quiet.

What changes

Steps, calories and battery become three rings around the edge, open at the bottom for the sports line. A light hour against a bold minute. Heart rate and FPL sit in two small chips.

On the watch

Rings use the arc sweep the dials already use. The photo is dimmed in advance, at build time and in the monthly set. Battery: same, or a touch lower (darker photo).

3

Matchday

A programme cover: photo above, team colour below

Borrowed from

Football matchday programmes and magazine covers; the big type of Pixel's Rushhour.

What changes

Giant numerals sit across the line between photo and band. All five readings sit in one scoreboard row. The sports line becomes the headline at the foot.

On the watch

The simplest to build: a solid band and text, no dials. Half the screen is a deep solid colour, so it lights fewer pixels than today. Battery: lower.

4

Glass

The photo stays vivid, frosted glass carries the data

Borrowed from

Apple's Liquid Glass numerals (watchOS 26 "Flow") and frosted cockpit displays.

What changes

The time sits on a frosted disc, with steps, calories and FPL on three glass tiles. Heart rate and battery go in a glass capsule up top, and the sports line on a glass bar.

On the watch

The watch can't blur live, so the frosting is baked into each photo: the built-in ones at build time, the monthly ones by the photo picker. Battery: same.

How to choose: pick a round 3 number (1 to 3), or mix (for example Quiet's photo-first layout with Clean Feed's bigger time).
